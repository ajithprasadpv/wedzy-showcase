Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Delivery

Wedzy plans are digital services. Your plan activates on your Wedzy account immediately after a successful payment. Open the app while signed in to the same account to use it. Nothing is shipped.

Cancellation

Every Wedzy plan is a one-time payment with no recurring charges, so there is nothing to cancel. You will never be charged again automatically.

Refunds

  • If you are not happy, email us within 7 days of purchase for a full refund. You don't need to give a reason.
  • If you were charged twice, or charged but the pass did not activate, we refund the extra or failed payment in full whenever you report it.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, netbanking or wallet) within 5–7 business days. When the refund is issued, the pass is removed from your account.

How to request a refund

Email ajithprasadpv@gmail.com with the email address of your Wedzy account and your payment ID or order ID, which are in the Razorpay receipt email.
