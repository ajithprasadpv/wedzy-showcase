Delete your Wedzy account

Wedding Planner by Wedzy · CipherKloud Technologies · Last updated 2 November 2026

You can delete your Wedzy account and the data linked to it at any time, from the app or by email. You don't need the app installed to ask by email.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Wedzy on your Android phone or iPhone and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm. If you signed in with a password, you'll be asked to enter it again.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Ask us by email

Email support@cipherkloud.com with the subject Delete my Wedzy account. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Wedzy, or tell us that address. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you. We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your Wedzy account and sign-in (email address, Google or Apple sign-in).
  • All wedding planning data synced to your account: names, wedding date, venue, tasks, budget, expenses, guests, vendors and photos.
  • The planning data stored on the phone where you delete the account. On other phones, it is removed when you open Wedzy there or uninstall the app.
  • Your Wedding Pass or Wedding Pass Plus. It can't be restored after the account is deleted.

What we keep

  • Payment records (order ID, amount, date and payment status) for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, usually 8 years.
  • Monthly voice command counts stored against an account ID. They contain no command text or personal details, and the ID can't be linked to you after the account is deleted.

We never keep the text of your voice commands. See the privacy policy for everything we collect.
