Questions

Good to know

Is Wedzy free?

Yes. Every planning feature is free, with 20 voice commands a month. Paid plans add more voice commands and remove ads.

Do I need a credit card to pay?

No. Buy a plan on this website with UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and others), a debit card, netbanking or a wallet. Payments are processed securely by Razorpay.

Will I be charged every month?

Never. Every plan is a one-time payment. Nothing renews automatically.

How does my plan reach the app?

Sign in on this website with the same account you use in the app. Your plan is active the moment the payment succeeds, on Android and iPhone.

Is my data private?

Your planning data belongs to you. It's tied to your account, and we never sell it or use it for advertising. Read the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Write to us within 7 days of buying for a full refund. See the refund policy.
